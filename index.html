Predictive Modeling  •  Python

Turnover Forecasting Model

A global manufacturer knew its attrition number after the fact and never before it. Retention spending went wherever the loudest complaint came from, which is a reasonable way to waste a budget.

I built a forecasting model in Python on top of consolidated Workday and survey data, testing which signals actually carried predictive weight rather than which ones people assumed did. The output gave leadership a forward-looking read they could target against, and the modeling process itself surfaced which data the organization had been collecting without ever using.

Roughly 85% accuracy, in production use for workforce planning
Research Design  •  Federal Data

Congressional District Intelligence Reports

Congressional districts generate enormous amounts of public data and almost nobody assembles it. Candidates run on instinct about their own district because the alternative is a research project nobody has funded.

I built a repeatable method that pulls district conditions from Census, BLS, and FRED, pairs them against legislative voting records from Congress.gov, and produces an indicator-based accountability analysis with a traceable citation behind every claim. Applied first to Washington's 4th District across healthcare, infrastructure and water, veterans, federal funding, and housing and food security, then to Michigan's 11th as a client deliverable to prove the model was portable.

Two full district reports plus condensed profiles and strategic briefs
Data Engineering  •  Automation

Compliance Analytics Pipeline Rebuild

Compliance and EEO reporting ran as a two-month manual assembly, which meant the numbers were stale by the time anyone read them and nobody could ask a follow-up question without restarting the whole thing.

I rebuilt it as an automated pipeline: sources pulled programmatically, validation built into the process rather than bolted on at the end, and the whole cycle rerunnable on demand. The point was not the hours saved. It was that a question could now be answered the same week it was asked.

Two months down to twenty hours, audit-ready on demand
Geospatial  •  API Collection

Precinct-Level Targeting System

A congressional campaign needed to know which precincts were worth its limited field hours, and the certified results that would answer that were spread across a state API and a pile of GIS shapefiles.

I built a precinct-level analysis system pulling from the VoteWA results API and Washington Secretary of State GIS-ready files, joined to district geography, then used certified primary results to build a general election viability case focused on Yakima and Benton counties. The same infrastructure fed volunteer task assignment and opposition research automations.

Precinct-level targeting driving field and fundraising strategy
Reporting  •  Power BI

Executive Dashboard Rebuild

Leadership reporting took roughly thirty hours to prepare, so it happened rarely and questions outside the standard set went unasked. Slow reporting does not just cost time, it quietly narrows what an organization is curious about.

I rebuilt the reporting layer in Power BI using M for transformation and DAX for measure logic, sitting on a consolidated data model rather than hand-assembled extracts. Preparation dropped to under ten minutes, and the visible change was behavioral: leaders started asking second and third questions because asking had stopped being expensive.

Thirty hours down to under ten minutes
Data Integration  •  Global Scale

Eleven-Country HR & Finance Data Integration

An international nonprofit ran HR, payroll, and finance across eleven countries and five continents in systems that had grown up independently and agreed on almost nothing, including what a headcount was.

I led the integration into a centralized reporting picture across Intacct, FinancialForce, and connected payroll systems, which meant resolving definitional conflicts before technical ones. Alongside it I coordinated multi-country compliance reporting with auditors and built the documentation that kept the definitions from drifting apart again.

One reporting picture across eleven countries, audit-clean
