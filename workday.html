Reporting, Prism ETL & Global HRIS

The Work That Makes an HRIS Trustworthy

Three years inside Workday at a global manufacturer, joining a few weeks after go-live and spending the whole tenure in stabilization and build rather than implementation.

Mostly the unglamorous kind of work that decides whether anyone believes the numbers: getting data in cleanly, getting reports out reliably, and making sure the right people can see the right things.

Inside Workday

What I built and ran

Prism Analytics

ETL & Data Consolidation

Three-plus years of Workday Prism Analytics ETL work, bringing multi-source datasets together so reporting drew on one consolidated picture instead of four disagreeing extracts. Combined Workday data with survey systems and external sources, which is where most accuracy problems live.

Data Operations

EIB Creation & Mass Data Loads

Created and managed EIBs for complex data operations across global teams, covering mass uploads, corrections, and the validation work that keeps a bad load from becoming a six-week cleanup. Built the checking step into the process rather than treating it as optional.

Reporting

Dashboards & Custom Reports

Designed custom dashboards in Workday and Power BI for leadership, taking reporting prep from roughly thirty hours down to under ten minutes. The Power BI side used M and DAX for transformation and measure logic.

The time saving mattered less than the effect on behavior. When a report takes thirty hours, leaders stop asking. When it takes ten minutes, they start asking better questions.

Compliance

Automated Compliance & EEO Pipelines

Automated the compliance and EEO analytics pipelines, cutting the cycle from two months to twenty hours. Supported audit readiness with reporting that could be regenerated on demand rather than reconstructed by hand each cycle.

Security

Security Groups & Role-Based Access

Improved Workday security configurations and role-based access to support compliance and audit requirements, working through who legitimately needs which data rather than defaulting to broad access.

Analytics

Predictive Turnover Model

Built a turnover forecasting model in Python reaching roughly 85% accuracy on top of the consolidated Workday data, giving leadership advance signal on workforce trends and a basis for targeting retention effort.

Beyond Workday

Community College System

HRIS Reporting & Compliance

Managed HRIS reporting and compliance across 550-plus employees, designed dashboards aligned to institutional planning, supported security and access management, and streamlined workflows including reduction-in-force protocols under union contracts.

Nonprofit & Campaign CRM

Constituent & Donor Data Systems

Ongoing work in Salesforce NPSP, EveryAction, and NGP VAN for nonprofit and campaign data operations, including CRM builds, integrations, and the data hygiene practices that keep a constituent database usable past its first year.
